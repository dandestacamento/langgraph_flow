## Cacio e pepe (15 minutes)

Toast cracked black pepper in a dry pan. Cook spaghetti, reserving a cup
of pasta water. Off the heat, toss the pasta with finely grated pecorino
and splashes of the starchy water until a glossy sauce forms.

## Aglio e olio (12 minutes)

Slice six garlic cloves thinly and warm them gently in olive oil until
pale gold - never brown. Add chilli flakes, then the cooked spaghetti
with pasta water. Finish with parsley and lemon zest.
