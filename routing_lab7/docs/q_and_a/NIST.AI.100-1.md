# Validation Q&A: NIST AI Risk Management Framework

Source: [NIST AI 100-1: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf).

These questions target specific statements in the document. References use the PDF viewer's page numbers, followed by the report's printed page numbers.

To pass, an answer must include every item in the expected answer and must not contradict it. Equivalent wording and additional correct details are acceptable. For numbered lists, check that all requested items are present. Check source references separately: a retrieved page is not necessarily a verified citation.

## 1. What two outcomes does the AI RMF aim for when addressing AI impacts?

**Expected answer:** Minimize negative impacts and identify opportunities to maximize positive impacts.

**Source:** PDF page 9 (report page 4).

## 2. What are the four AI RMF Core functions?

**Expected answer:** GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE.

**Source:** PDF page 25 (report page 20).

## 3. Assuming a governance structure is in place, must the AI RMF functions be performed in a fixed order?

**Expected answer:** No.

**Context:** They may be performed in any order as appropriate for the user.

**Source:** PDF page 26 (report page 21).

## 4. Which three functions is GOVERN designed to be infused throughout?

**Expected answer:** MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE.

**Source:** PDF page 25 (report page 20).

## 5. The outcomes of MAP form the basis for which two functions?

**Expected answer:** MEASURE and MANAGE.

**Source:** PDF page 30 (report page 25).

## 6. According to the MEASURE function, when should AI systems be tested?

**Expected answer:** Before deployment and regularly while in operation.

**Source:** PDF page 33 (report page 28).

## 7. What four risk-response options are listed in MANAGE 1.3?

**Expected answer:** Mitigating, transferring, avoiding, and accepting risk.

**Source:** PDF page 37 (report page 32).

## 8. According to MANAGE 4.3, to whom should incidents and errors be communicated?

**Expected answer:** Relevant AI actors, including affected communities.

**Source:** PDF page 38 (report page 33).

## 9. Does the AI RMF prescribe an organization's risk tolerance?

**Expected answer:** No.

**Context:** Risk tolerance is contextual and depends on the application or use case.

**Source:** PDF page 12 (report page 7).

## 10. What seven characteristics of trustworthy AI are listed in the framework?

**Expected answer:** Valid and reliable; safe; secure and resilient; accountable and transparent; explainable and interpretable; privacy-enhanced; and fair with harmful bias managed.

**Source:** PDF page 17 (report page 12).

## 11. Which questions do transparency, explainability, and interpretability respectively help answer?

**Expected answer:** Transparency addresses what happened; explainability addresses how a decision was made; interpretability addresses why the decision was made and its meaning or context to the user.

**Source:** PDF page 22 (report page 17).

## 12. What three major categories of AI bias does NIST identify?

**Expected answer:** Systemic, computational and statistical, and human-cognitive bias.

**Source:** PDF page 23 (report page 18).

## 13. An AI RMF use-case profile is based on which three aspects of the framework user?

**Expected answer:** The user's requirements, risk tolerance, and resources.

**Source:** PDF page 38 (report page 33).

## 14. What is the difference between an AI RMF Current Profile and a Target Profile?

**Expected answer:** A Current Profile describes current AI risk-management outcomes; a Target Profile describes the outcomes needed to achieve desired risk-management goals.

**Source:** PDF page 38 (report page 33).

## 15. What should be employed when choosing trustworthiness metrics and their precise threshold values?

**Expected answer:** Human judgment.

**Source:** PDF page 17 (report page 12).
