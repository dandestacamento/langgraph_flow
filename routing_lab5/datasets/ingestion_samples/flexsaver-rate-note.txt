FlexSaver Account: how the bonus rate works

The FlexSaver savings account pays a base rate of 3.10% p.a. plus a bonus
of 1.65% p.a. (5.75% p.a. total under current bonus conditions) in any
month where BOTH conditions are met:

1. You deposit at least $200 into the FlexSaver account during the month.
2. You make no more than one withdrawal from the account in that month.

If either condition is missed, only the base rate applies for that month;
the bonus is never clawed back retroactively from earlier months. Interest
is calculated daily on the closing balance and credited on the first
business day of the following month.

Note on rate tiers: balances above $250,000 earn the base rate only -
the bonus rate applies to the portion of the balance up to $250,000.

The FlexSaver account has no monthly account-keeping fee.
