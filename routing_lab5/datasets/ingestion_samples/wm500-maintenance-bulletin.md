# WM-500 Washing Machine - Field Maintenance Bulletin 2026-03

This bulletin supplements the WM-500 documentation with newly catalogued
fault codes and maintenance procedures reported by service centres.

## New fault code E77 - door-lock jam

Error E77 indicates a door-lock jam: the door latch motor stalls before the
lock engages. Power-cycle the machine, then open and firmly re-close the
door. If E77 persists, the latch assembly (part DL-19) needs replacement -
do not force the door, as that bends the strike plate.

## Preventative maintenance schedule

Wipe the door seal monthly and run a maintenance wash at 60 degrees every
eight weeks. Service centres report that following this schedule roughly
halves E77 latch-assembly replacements over the machine's life.

## Warranty note for E77 repairs

A latch assembly replacement for error E77 is covered under the WM-500's
standard two-year warranty, including labour, when the preventative
maintenance schedule above has been followed.
